# Pure sampler case study protocol

Frozen design for the 26 September 2026 study, specified before measured plans.

- 60 new endpoint-feasible environments; 10 independent draws in each of six strata (central, offset, distant cylinder crossed with normalized RL clearance 0.2 and 0.8).
- 10 stochastic planning repeats per environment and method: 1,800 plans. Fixed sample size; no significance-based stopping. Smoke runs use another seed and are excluded.
- Same Panda model, collision scene, exact start/goal joint states, RRTConnect, 3 s planning budget, one planning attempt, no path simplification, identical response adapters and joint-space edge resolution for all methods. Plan-only; no robot execution.
- Pure `cartesian_ik`: truncated Cartesian GMM proposal followed by IK. Pure `joint_projected`: local Jacobian mapping around precomputed IK anchors, including null-space noise; no online Cartesian IK rescue. Both use `proposal=gmm`, covariance corridor, cutoff 2.0, zero uniform/cartesian mixture fractions.
- OMPL baseline: default joint-space sampling with empty path constraints. It is uniform in joint coordinates, not Cartesian workspace volume. Collision and joint bounds still apply.
- MoveIt's `allow_constraint_sampler_fallback=false` disables its otherwise hidden default draws after three custom failures. Exhausted samplers retry until timeout/cancellation, then fail. Strict mode currently requires one planning attempt. A synthetic model with unreachable component means tests this path before measurements.
- The two custom modes use the same hard positional corridor (union of covariance-axis-aligned boxes enclosing the cutoff ellipsoids); the unrestricted baseline has a larger feasible region. Baseline contrasts measure complete planning configurations, not only a change in proposal density. No fixed-orientation path constraint is imposed: Cartesian IK proposals have the requested orientation; projected proposals use a translational Jacobian and may vary orientation. Both are subjected to the same positional/collision constraints. Sample targets obey the ellipsoidal cutoff; tree interpolation and returned paths may enter box corners outside the ellipsoids. A radius of 2 in three dimensions contains about 73.85% of an untruncated Gaussian, not 95%; feasibility filtering further changes the accepted distribution.
- Each environment freezes the reproduced/deformed model, checkpoint identity, policy action scale, reference curves, endpoints and complete scene. Endpoint IK/collision infeasibility is the only design rejection. A custom method with endpoints outside its corridor counts as a failure. No selection based on planning outcome.
- Shared offline audit interpolates returned trajectories at a maximum 0.01 MoveIt group-distance step; checks bounds, collisions, feasibility and applicable constraints; records whole-robot/world clearance, joint and end-effector path length, trajectory duration and the EE polyline. This is sampled validation, not a continuous collision certificate. Whole-world clearance includes the table; cylinder-specific EE-point clearance is reported separately.
- Sample/path visualization disabled during timing. Model preparation is performed once per environment and shared by the two custom methods; the recorded one-shot preparation cost is added to their end-to-end latency, but not to unrestricted OMPL. Stage measurements are wall-clock observations, not independent repeated preparation measurements. Nested sampling/IK/validity timers must not be summed.
- Primary outcomes: audited success and PAR2 (successful action wall time capped at 3 s; any failure costs 6 s). Also report raw action wall time, MoveIt time and pipeline latency, including budget overshoot and setup overhead. Three pairwise contrasts for both outcomes form one six-test Holm family at 0.05.
- Independent units are environments, not the 600 repeated plans per method. Paired stratified cluster bootstrap (20,000 replicates, 95% percentile intervals) retains all methods/repeats within each environment. Paired label/sign permutations operate at environment level (100,000 draws). Pointwise confidence intervals are not simultaneous intervals. Degenerate all-success bootstrap intervals do not establish perfect population reliability.
- Secondary path quality compares only matched successful environment/repeat pairs; report retained counts and selection bias. No confirmatory claims based on these exploratory endpoints.
- Selection prioritizes reliability. A faster method should be within a predeclared 5 percentage-point success margin and show at least 10% lower failure-penalized runtime with uncertainty considered. Otherwise report an inconclusive or environment-specific recommendation. Do not interpret nonsignificance as equivalence or guarantee statistical significance.
- Seeds fix environment generation, execution order and custom proposal streams. OMPL and the IK plugin have their own RNG streams; repeats are matched problem instances, not identical underlying random numbers. Newly generated environments are not proven absent from the RL policy's training distribution.

Methods references: [OMPL benchmarking](https://ompl.kavrakilab.org/core/benchmark.html), [paired permutation testing](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.permutation_test.html), [paired bootstrap resampling](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.bootstrap.html).

The manifest, frozen environments and append-only JSONL results support resuming an interrupted run. Do not pool the older mixed/fallback-permitted comparisons with this strict study.


## Independent path-quality confirmation

Specified after completion of the original 1,800-plan study and before any confirmation plans. The original primary sampler comparison was inconclusive for superiority in success/PAR2. Its exploratory matched-success results suggested shorter projected paths. To check that observation independently, run 60 **new** environments (seed 20260927; ten per original stratum), five repeats, and all three methods: 900 additional plans. All robot, model, sampler, planning and audit settings stay fixed.

The confirmation primary family has two directional hypotheses, chosen before confirmation: Cartesian-minus-projected mean joint path length and EE path length are positive. Compare environment-average differences over matched successful repeats; one-sided environment sign permutations, 100,000 draws, Holm across two tests at 0.05. Report 95% paired cluster intervals and retained environments/pairs. Joint length is MoveIt's weighted sum of absolute joint changes, not the Euclidean joint-vector length. Report this conditional-on-success estimand and selection bias explicitly.

Success, PAR2, stage timings and unrestricted-baseline contrasts remain secondary in this confirmation phase. Do not pool p-values with the original study or add further runs based on the result. A confirmed joint-motion reduction can justify a **path-quality-based** engineering selection; it does not establish greater reliability, faster planning, or suitability for untested EV orientation constraints. Report an unconfirmed result if confirmation fails.


Completed artifacts: [case study report, data and figures](case_studies/2026-09-26-pure-sampling/README.md). The selection is based on independently confirmed joint-travel reduction; speed/reliability superiority was not established.

## Subsequent clearance sensitivity replay

The [0.5 / 0.7 replay protocol](CLEARANCE_REPLAY.md) reuses both original cohorts and changes only the requested RL clearance. It preserves the original goal heights and exact joint endpoints, verifies the live collision scene, and reports paired before/after effects separately for the reduced-clearance and increased-clearance groups. Reused environments do not provide another independent confirmation. The replay protocol also records the scene-restoration defect that invalidated its first attempt and the complete restart after correcting it.
